# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Eval Forge to be a welcoming place for everyone who wants to test, compare, or improve AI models, whatever their background, experience, or opinions. Be kind, be specific, and assume good faith.

## What we expect

- Be respectful, even when you disagree about a model, a result, or a prompt.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Credit others when you build on their work.
- Keep discussion on the project and its content.

## What is not okay

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or spam.
- Publishing private information about someone without consent.
- Submitting content that is deceptive, such as made-up results presented as real.
- Any other conduct a reasonable person would find inappropriate in a shared project.

## Scope

This applies to the repository, its issues and pull requests, and any official Eval Forge spaces.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainers through the repository's GitHub account at [github.com/eval-forge](https://github.com/eval-forge). Reports are reviewed confidentially.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, and pull requests that break this code, and may restrict or remove participants who repeatedly do so.
